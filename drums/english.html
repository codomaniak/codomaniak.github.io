🥁 Specialised Branch · Orchestral / Latin / World

Multi‑percussionist competencies beyond the drumset

Mallet Keyboard

Vibes, marimba, xylo — 4‑mallet independence, dampening, interval skipping

Timpani

Tonal accuracy (pedaling), stroke types (legato, staccato, portato), muffling

Accessories

Triangle damping, tambourine thumb roll, cymbal selection, conga/bongo hand technique

World Rhythm Vocabulary

Samba Batucada, West African Djembe (bass/slap/tone), Darbuka, orchestral excerpts

📌 How to use this map

  • Self‑assessment — mark a level only when you can meet its Pass evidence
  • Asymmetric time — Technical Execution is hardest: give it most pad/kit minutes and stretch each plan “month” (often 6–10 weeks)
  • Start musical domains early — Perception & Musicality are easier; train them from day 1 in shorter blocks, not after technique is “finished”
  • Groove library — style/groove first, then song applications + form adaptations (see planner; samples in styles.md)
  • Measurable goals — BPM ladders (+5 only when clean); named songs for musical work
  • Band audition — prioritise Musicality & Professional domains; a reliable Novice often beats a difficult Expert
