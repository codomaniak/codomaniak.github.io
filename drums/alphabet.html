How it works

  • Binary letters A–P are four-part 16th-note beats (1 e & a); ternary letters Q–X are three-part triplet beats (1 ka ta).
  • Play every slot with the stroke types — accents and Moeller prep (tap/up) on “rest” slots.
  • | full · ↓ down · - tap · ↑ up
  • Kit notation: binary unit X-g-g-g- at Grid 16 (×2 per bar); ternary unit X-x-x- at Grid 12 (×2 per bar).
  • Word mode accepts letters A–X.
