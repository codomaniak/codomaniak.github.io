🔸 Ternary Letters (Triplet Grid)
These 8 letters (Q through X) represent every combination within a single quarter-note beat divided into three triplet eighth notes (1 ka ta).
Stroke glyphs for the trainer: `|` full · `↓` down · `↑` up · `-` tap

Note: kit patterns use unit `X-x-x-` (6 slots). **Grid 12** bar = unit × 2 (12 slots).
